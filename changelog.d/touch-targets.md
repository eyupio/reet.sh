- **Bigger touch targets in the terminal and the chat queue**: on a touch screen the terminal's toolbar, extra-key row,
  header controls, Manage menu, copy and paste menu and footer button are now 44 px, and so are the buttons in a chat's
  queue of waiting messages (Send now, Stop and send, Retry, Edit, Remove, Save and Cancel) and the box you tick before
  a retry. A very small phone (320 px wide) shows one terminal row fewer, because the larger controls take the room.
