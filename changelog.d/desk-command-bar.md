- **A command bar on a desk**: under a desk's panes there is now a field that runs a command in the focused pane's
  shell. Before you type, it offers what you might do next: `cd` into each folder of the folder the shell is in, and
  the commands you ran lately or saved. As you type, it completes the command or the folder. Tab takes a suggestion,
  Enter runs the line, and Alt+Shift+K moves to the bar from anywhere on the desk. Nothing runs until you press Enter
  or Run.
