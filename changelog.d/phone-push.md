- **Notifications that stopped are noticed and repaired, open the right place, and can play a sound**: a phone whose
  push subscription the browser or push service dropped now says "Notifications stopped reaching this phone." on
  **Notifications → On this device**, with a **Repair** button, instead of going quiet; the controller keeps such a
  device marked rather than forgetting it, and sends it nothing until it is repaired. A tap on a notification opens
  that session on its chat or terminal, in the reet window already open, and a session removed meanwhile opens the
  sessions list with a line saying so. **Play a sound** (off by default, per device) adds a short, quiet tone while
  reet is open in front.
