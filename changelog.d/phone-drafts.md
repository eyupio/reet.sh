- **Drafts survive a reload, and the chat box stays typeable while it reconnects**: what you have typed but not sent in
  a chat, in the terminal's Compose box, and in a chat's queue of waiting messages is now kept in this browser, so a
  reload or a phone browser that drops the tab does not lose it. It comes back without taking focus and never over text
  you have already typed. It is text only (no pictures), kept per account and session, for 14 days and at most 20
  drafts, and is deleted when you send it, empty it or sign out. While a chat reconnects you can keep typing; Send waits
  and says "Reconnecting. Your message is kept." A queue that comes back after a reload waits for Retry rather than
  sending itself.
