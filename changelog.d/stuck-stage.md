- **A Factory stage no longer gets stuck after its agent's turn fails**: an instruction from Gaffer or from **Tell the
  agent…** now reaches the agent after a failed turn, where it used to be held in the session's queue with no way to
  retry it. A stage that waits for you also offers **Restart stage…**, which asks first, ends the stuck session
  and starts the stage again in a new one in the same folder and branch. In the session, the decision can be folded
  away with **Minimise**, a long instruction starts at three lines, and the composer stays on screen at phone, tablet
  and desktop sizes. On the Factory page, what waits for you is shown above the skill suggestions. In a new outcome, a
  crew tier you changed has a **Back to your saved crew** link, and a stage's **Use Standard** button is now **Move back
  to Standard tier**, since it moves that one stage and does not undo a model you picked for a tier.
